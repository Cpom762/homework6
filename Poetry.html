Poetry

Monochrome

I glance at—
the canvas, blank slate on the wall

I couldn't—
take chances, I never did fall

I glanced at—
the canvas, and people were there

Circumstances—
and anguish, no reason to stare…

My life is full of paintings—
that honestly I'd rather ignore…

A gallery of faces—
the world must think I'm rather deplor…

—able…

Never knew—
I’d see the day I see the world in tone
Maybe blue, maybe stone or no heart at all?

Hey I knew—
a rainy cloud couldn't weather a storm

Maybe me—
maybe you—
maybe everything is monochrome

Shades of blue—
don't affect me in my world at all

Baby blue—
different colors don't exist on the wall

Never knew—
I’d see the day I see the world in tone
Is it blue?
Is it stone or no heart at all?

Guess I knew—
a rainy cloud couldn't weather the storm

Maybe me—
maybe you—
maybe everything is
Monochrome

Where's the art?
Where's the tone?
Where did my feelings go?

In my heart?
I'm alone
Am I ready to go?

From the start
What was sown?
Where did my feelings go?

Was I really alone?

Or are they Monochrome?

Never knew—
I'd see the day I see the world in tone
In my heart, in my soul—
Was I really alone?

Should've known—
I’d see the day I turned my heart into stone
Empty heart
Lost the art
My world is Monochrome.
